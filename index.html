The name

Sillage is the trail a perfume leaves in the air after someone has walked past. You never smell your own. We make five, each one built to stay in a room a little longer than you do.

The atelier

Slow on purpose

Every batch rests for six weeks before it is filtered, so the notes have time to settle into each other. We fill by hand, in small runs, and number every bottle.
